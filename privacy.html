Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who uses this app

Finance Tracker is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, who authorises it on their own Google account.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app uses the Gmail read-only permission to read bank and credit card transaction alert emails in one Gmail label. It cannot send, change or delete email.

How the data is used

From each alert it extracts the transaction details (amount, merchant, card or account, date) and stores them in the owner's own Google Cloud project to show the owner their spending. Email content is not used for anything else.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising or to train any model.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept in the owner's Google Cloud project until the owner deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
